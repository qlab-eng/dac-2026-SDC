On-Device Clinical Speech Agent Contest at DAC 2026(current)

Introduction

The On-Device Clinical Speech Agent Contest at DAC 2026 challenges participants to design and deploy an edge-based speech-language assessment agent for clinical screening applications. The agent must process speech recordings entirely on a resource-constrained edge device, producing clinically meaningful assessments without transmitting any raw audio or transcript data to the cloud.

Clinical speech analysis is critical for early screening of conditions such as Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias (ADRD) and childhood developmental language delays. However, existing cloud-based speech analysis pipelines raise serious privacy concerns, as clinical speech recordings contain highly sensitive health information. On-device processing addresses this by keeping all data local, making it suitable for IoT-based healthcare deployments such as smart home assistants for elderly monitoring and wearable devices for pediatric assessment.

Participants will build an end-to-end pipeline that integrates automatic speech recognition (ASR), linguistic feature extraction, and a language model-based clinical classifier, all running on a given edge platform under strict compute and memory budgets. Winning teams will be invited to attend DAC 2026 to present their solutions and receive awards.
