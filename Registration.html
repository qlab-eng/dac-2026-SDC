On-Device Clinical Speech Agent Contest at DAC 2026(current)

Registration

To participate in the On-Device Clinical Speech Agent Contest at DAC 2026, please complete the registration form below. Registration is free and open to teams from around the world.

Registration Requirements
  • Each team may have up to 5 members.
  • At least one team member must be affiliated with an academic institution or research organization.
  • Please indicate which track(s) you plan to enter: Track 1 (ADRD Screening), Track 2 (Child Language Assessment), or both.
  • All team members must agree to the contest rules and data usage terms.
Registration Form

Registration will open soon. Please check back later or follow the News page for updates.

After Registration

Upon successful registration, you will receive:

  • Access to the contest dataset (download links and usage instructions).
  • The baseline implementation and Docker container specification.
  • An invitation to the contest communication channel for Q&A and announcements.
